\section{Research Interests}
Multimodal learning and representation learning, with a focus on vision-language models,
text–video retrieval, GUI agents, compositional image retrieval, video question answering,
and applications of large language and multimodal models. Research interests include
cross-modal alignment, generative caption utilization, bridging semantic gaps between
modalities, and domain-specific AI for medical applications.
